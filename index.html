Yumi Café — Privacy Policy

Developer: Paracord Studio · Effective date: 28 October 2026 (updated the same day: game statistics, section 6)

In short: Yumi Café is a cooking game for children and families (rated PEGI 3). We do not ask for names, e-mail addresses, photos, contacts or any other personal information, and there are no accounts to create, no chats and no in-app purchases. Game progress stays on the device and, only if a Google Play Games profile is signed in, in its cloud save. The weekly league uses a random player ID made by the game (no name, no e-mail) stored with our game-server provider Unity Gaming Services; other players only see a made-up name like "Captain Muffin" and the avatar picture the child chose. The game shows optional rewarded video ads, served in child-directed mode by our ad partner Appodeal, which automatically receives limited technical information to deliver those ads. To see which levels are too hard and what children enjoy, the game sends anonymous game statistics (no name, no advertising ID) to Google Analytics for Firebase.

1. Who we are

Yumi Café (the "Game") is developed by Paracord Studio ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your child's data, contact us at paracordstudio7@gmail.com.

2. Children's privacy

  • The Game is designed for all ages, including young children. It is treated as directed to children under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR rules for children and Google Play's Families Policy.
  • We never knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game has no registration, no password, no chat, no friend lists and no way for a child to type, enter or share personal information. The weekly league (section 5) shows other players only a made-up name from a fixed word list and a picture from a fixed set — never anything a player wrote.
  • The Game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions, and does not use the device's advertising ID.
  • Links that leave the Game (this policy, support e-mail, store rating page) are behind a parental gate that a young child cannot pass.

3. Information stored on the device

The Game saves your progress locally on the device: completed levels and stars, coins, unlocked items, the chosen avatar, chosen language and sound settings. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

4. Google Play Games cloud save (optional)

If a Google Play Games profile is signed in on the device, the Game also keeps a copy of the same progress in that profile's saved games, so it is not lost on a new device. Signing in is never required; a parent can sign in from the parents' corner. The saved game is stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we do not receive it.

5. Weekly league (online)

  • To place players in weekly groups of up to 30, the Game connects to Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Code and Cloud Save by Unity Technologies), which processes the data below for us as a service provider.
  • On first use, Unity Authentication creates a random player ID for the Game on this device. It is not linked to a name, e-mail address, phone number or the device's advertising ID. If a Google Play Games profile is signed in, the player ID is linked to that profile so the same league player is found again after a reinstall or on another device.
  • For each won level the Game sends the level number, stars, moves, mistakes, number of guests, combos and the time it was won. The server keeps the player's league tier, weekly group, Chef Points, the levels counted that week, the week's result, and the number of the chosen avatar picture.
  • Other players in the same group see only the place, the Chef Points, a made-up name chosen by the player ID from a fixed list of words (for example "Captain Muffin") and the avatar picture. They never see the player ID.
  • The data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), is used only to run the league, and is not sold or used for advertising. Unity processes it under the Unity Privacy Policy. Without an internet connection the Game plays normally; the league simply waits.
  • League notifications (optional). Only while the Notifications switch in the Game's settings is on and Android allows notifications, the Game asks Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging for a push token for this device and sends it to the league server with the device's time zone offset and the Game's language. The server uses them only to send a short notice when another player takes the player's 1st place or the places that move up (at most one a day, only in the daytime of the player's time zone). Turning the switch off deletes the token on the device and on the server. The notice contains no name and no message from other players. Firebase processes the token under the Firebase privacy information. Game statistics are described in section 6.

6. Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

  • To learn which levels are too hard, where children stop playing and which parts of the Game they use, the Game sends anonymous game statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase (Google), which processes them for us as a service provider. We only look at totals and averages; we never try to identify a child.
  • What the Game sends: a level started, won or left, with its number, moves, mistakes, time and stars; steps of the first lesson; whether a reward-video button was shown, tapped and watched to the end (and in which part of the Game); coins earned and spent and the item bought with them; use of the hint and undo buttons; which screens were opened (shop, league, daily quests, lucky spin, "My levels"); changed settings (sound, music, vibration, notifications, mistake explanations); and whether the Game was opened from one of its notifications. These are numbers and fixed names only — never anything a child typed or said.
  • What the Firebase SDK adds automatically: a random app-instance ID created for this installation of the Game, the operating system, language, app version, session start and play time, first open and app updates, and the country or region derived from the IP address. City-level location and detailed device data (such as the device model) are switched off, and Google Analytics does not store the IP address.
  • What it does not use: the device's advertising ID (switched off in the Game), names, e-mail addresses or any account. Ad personalization, Google signals and every option to share the data with Google for its own purposes (product improvement, benchmarking, technical support, account specialists) are switched off, so the statistics are not used for advertising or combined with other Google data. They are not sold.
  • The data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and processed under the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy. How long it is kept is in section 10.

7. Advertising

  • The Game shows rewarded video ads only, and only when a player taps a clearly marked button (for example, "x3" coins after a level, "x1.5" after a party level, or "+100" coins in the shop). There are no banners and no ads that interrupt play. Watching an ad is always optional.
  • Ads are served through the Appodeal mediation platform and the ad networks it works with. We tell Appodeal that the Game is child-directed, so only contextual (non-personalized) ads suitable for children are requested. No behavioural advertising, profiling or cross-app tracking is permitted for this Game.
  • To deliver ads, measure that they were shown, limit how often they are shown and prevent fraud, the ad SDK automatically collects and shares the technical information listed in section 8 with Appodeal and its ad partners.

9. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade any information.
  • We do not use personalized or behavioural advertising, and game statistics are never used for advertising.
  • We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings, precise location or contacts.
  • We do not let players write to each other or see anything another player typed.
  • We do not offer in-app purchases or chat with other players.

10. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on the device until the Game is uninstalled (and in the Play Games saved game while that profile keeps it). League data is kept with Unity Gaming Services while the league runs: a player's tier and avatar number stay with the player, and the weekly groups (places and Chef Points) are deleted about four weeks after their week ends. The push token is kept only while notifications are switched on. Game statistics are kept by Google Analytics as single events for 2 months; the app-instance ID and its data are kept for up to 14 months after the Game was last played, after which only totals remain. They are not linked to a name or account, so we cannot pick out one child's statistics; uninstalling the Game or clearing its data starts a new app-instance ID, and the old one's data expires as above. Parents may ask us at any time to delete the league data of their child's player — write to us and we will delete it within 30 days. Technical data received by Appodeal and its partners is kept only as long as they need it for the purposes above, as described in their policies; we will forward deletion requests to them.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, UK GDPR or COPPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Write to us and we will help; league data is handled with Unity Gaming Services, game statistics with Google Analytics for Firebase and ad data with our ad partners.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the store listing's "What's new" notes.
