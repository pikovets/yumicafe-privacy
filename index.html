Yumi Café — Privacy Policy

Developer: Paracord Studio · Effective date: 17 October 2026

In short: Yumi Café is a cooking game for children and families (rated PEGI 3). We do not ask for names, e-mail addresses, photos, contacts or any other personal information, and there are no in-game accounts, chats or in-app purchases. Game progress is saved on the device and, only if the device is signed in to Google Play Games, also in that Google Play Games account so it can continue on another device. The game shows optional rewarded video ads, served in child-directed mode by our ad partner Appodeal, which automatically receives limited technical information to deliver those ads.

1. Who we are

Yumi Café (the "Game") is developed by Paracord Studio ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your child's data, contact us at paracordstudio7@gmail.com.

2. Children's privacy

  • The Game is designed for all ages, including young children. It is treated as directed to children under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR rules for children and Google Play's Families Policy.
  • We never knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game has no registration of its own, no social features and no way for a child to enter or share personal information. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional and is never required to play (see section 3).
  • The Game does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions, and does not use the device's advertising ID.
  • Links that leave the Game (this policy, support e-mail, store rating page) and the Google Play Games sign-in button are behind a parental gate that a young child cannot pass.

3. Game progress: on the device and, optionally, in Google Play Games

  • The Game saves progress locally on the device: the level reached, coins, unlocked and chosen items, which tips were already shown, chosen language and sound settings. This information is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.
  • Google Play Games cloud save (optional). If the device is signed in to a Google Play Games profile (set up by the device owner, or by a parent with Google Family Link), the Game uses it automatically; a parent can also sign in from the parents' area, behind the parental gate. The Game then keeps a copy of the progress listed above (not the language or sound settings) in that account's Google Play Games saved games, so the same account can continue on another device. The Game receives the account's Google Play Games player identity (player ID and display name) only to read and write that copy.
  • The saved game is stored by Google, in an area that only this Game can read or write, and is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not receive it on our own servers. The Google Play Games software also collects analytics and diagnostics to keep its service working. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Without a Google Play Games profile the Game works exactly the same; progress then stays only on the device.

4. Advertising

  • The Game shows rewarded video ads only, and only when a player taps a clearly marked button (for example, the coins ×3 button after a level or "+100" coins in the shop). There are no banners and no ads that interrupt play. Watching an ad is always optional.
  • Ads are served through the Appodeal mediation platform and the ad networks it works with. We tell Appodeal that the Game is child-directed, so only contextual (non-personalized) ads suitable for children are requested. No behavioural advertising, profiling or cross-app tracking is permitted for this Game.
  • To deliver ads, measure that they were shown, limit how often they are shown and prevent fraud, the ad SDK automatically collects and shares the technical information listed in section 5 with Appodeal and its ad partners.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade any information.
  • We do not use personalized or behavioural advertising.
  • We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings, precise location or contacts.
  • We do not offer in-app purchases or chat with other players.

7. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep any personal data on our own servers. Game progress stays on the device until the Game is uninstalled. A Google Play Games saved game stays in that Google account until it is deleted there: in the Google Play Games app (Profile → Settings → delete Play Games account and data, or the data of a single game) or in the Google Account. Technical data received by Appodeal and its partners is kept only as long as they need it for the purposes above, as described in their policies. Parents may contact us at any time to ask questions or to request that we forward a deletion request to our ad partners; we will answer within 30 days.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, UK GDPR or COPPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not hold personal data ourselves, most requests are handled together with our ad partners — write to us and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the store listing's "What's new" notes.
